Methodology

1 · Prose figures

Market data is pulled from live public sources with cross-source checks — unconfirmed figures come back null rather than guessed. The AI writes its analysis constrained to that data only. Code then extracts every figure from the AI's writing and reconciles it against the source with a 3% tolerance for rounding. Mismatches are flagged unverified, and each flag docks a point off the AI's stated confidence.

2 · Chart candles

Candles are checked for internal consistency, must fall inside the 52-week range for sub-year views, and the latest close must reconcile with the live price. Failures are marked ⚑.

3 · Technicals by construction

Return, volatility, drawdown and trend are computed in page code from the audited candles — the AI never touches them.

4 · The AI's own judgement

Every verdict is logged in your browser with the price at the time of the call; re-checking performance marks each call right or wrong. Each analysis also states what would prove it wrong.

Known limits

Prices come from web sources, not an exchange feed, so figures can lag and candles are indicative rather than tick-accurate — which is exactly why the audits exist. Verification confirms consistency with pulled sources, not absolute truth. Nothing here is investment advice.
